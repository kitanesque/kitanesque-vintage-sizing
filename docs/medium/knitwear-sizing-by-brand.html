Knitwear Sizing by Brand

119 knitted pieces from 84 labels, measured flat with the label in hand, in stock and sold. Among the labels we have measured three or more times, Repeat came out roomiest against the UK standard for the size we listed it as (+3.5″ on average) and F&F closest to it (0″).

Labels with three or more measured pieces get their own row. “Listed as” is the UK size on each piece’s own listing, judged from its flat measurements, never a calculation. “Bust vs UK standard” is how much bigger or smaller the bust measured than the standard for that listed size; knitwear is cut with ease, so a jumper that is meant to skim the body measures a little over. Women’s pieces, inches throughout; the bust is measured flat under the arms and doubled.

How to use this

  • Find your usual UK size in the second table and read the bust range next to it: that is what we have listed at your size.
  • A label marked “runs small” in the first table is one to size up in; “roomy” is cut for layering or an oversized look.
  • Before you buy, open the listing: each piece carries its own measurements, and Will it fit? checks them against your size.
  • Which cashmere labels are worth buying second-hand, and at what price: our cashmere guide.

Questions people ask

Does vintage knitwear run true to size? Mostly, with a lean towards roomy. Across the 119 knitted pieces we have measured, the bust came out +1.5″ against the UK standard for the size we listed on average, and 31 of them were within 1.5 inches of it. Among the labels we have measured three or more times, Repeat came out roomiest against the UK standard for the size we listed it as (+3.5″ on average) and F&F closest to it (0″).

Does Marks & Spencer knitwear run small or true to size? On the 6 Marks & Spencer pieces we measured, the bust came out +1″ against the UK standard for the size we listed on average, which we would call close to the label. The 2 pieces we listed as UK 14–16 measured 36–41″ round, against a standard of about 40″ for that size. Measured flat on each piece, not the brand's chart.

Does F&F knitwear run small or true to size? On the 5 F&F pieces we measured, the bust came out 0″ against the UK standard for the size we listed on average, which we would call close to the label. The 2 pieces we listed as UK 8–10 measured 35–38″ round, against a standard of about 34″ for that size. Measured flat on each piece, not the brand's chart.

Where do these numbers come from? From our own listings. Every knitted piece we sell is measured flat before it goes online, and this page reads those measurements straight from the catalogue, sold pieces included. It rebuilds itself when a piece is added or sold; the newest piece in it was listed on 19 November 2026.
