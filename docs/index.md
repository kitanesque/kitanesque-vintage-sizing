# Kitanesque guides and measured data

Text copies of the guides on [kitanesque.co.uk](https://kitanesque.co.uk/), a UK shop for vintage and second-hand clothing. Every figure comes from pieces we measured flat, by hand. The live pages, with photos and today's numbers, are at [kitanesque.co.uk/guides/](https://kitanesque.co.uk/guides/).

- [What Counts as Low-Rise? Rise Measured on Vintage Jeans](what-counts-as-low-rise.md): Front rise measured on our vintage jeans: where low-rise starts and ends. Live page: [kitanesque.co.uk/what-counts-as-low-rise/](https://kitanesque.co.uk/what-counts-as-low-rise/)
- [Jeans Sizing by Brand](jeans-sizing-by-brand.md): Vintage jeans by brand: label waist against the tape. Live page: [kitanesque.co.uk/jeans-sizing-by-brand/](https://kitanesque.co.uk/jeans-sizing-by-brand/)
- [Knitwear Sizing by Brand](knitwear-sizing-by-brand.md): Second-hand knitwear by brand: bust measured against the label size. Live page: [kitanesque.co.uk/knitwear-sizing-by-brand/](https://kitanesque.co.uk/knitwear-sizing-by-brand/)
- [Vintage Dress Sizing by Brand: Label vs Tape](dress-sizing-by-brand.md): Dresses by brand: measured bust, waist and length against the label. Live page: [kitanesque.co.uk/dress-sizing-by-brand/](https://kitanesque.co.uk/dress-sizing-by-brand/)
- [Is It Real Cashmere? What the Labels and the Fabric Tell You](is-it-real-cashmere.md): How to check cashmere, with the labels from our own stock. Live page: [kitanesque.co.uk/is-it-real-cashmere/](https://kitanesque.co.uk/is-it-real-cashmere/)
- [How to Date Vintage Clothing from Its Label](date-vintage-clothing-by-label.md): Dating vintage clothing from its labels, RN numbers and care symbols. Live page: [kitanesque.co.uk/date-vintage-clothing-by-label/](https://kitanesque.co.uk/date-vintage-clothing-by-label/)
- [How to Wash Vintage Cashmere, Silk and Wool: What the Care Labels Say](how-to-wash-vintage-clothes-care-labels.md): What the care labels on our vintage pieces actually say. Live page: [kitanesque.co.uk/how-to-wash-vintage-clothes-care-labels/](https://kitanesque.co.uk/how-to-wash-vintage-clothes-care-labels/)
- [What Makes Cashmere Expensive, and When the Cheap Stuff Is Fine](second-hand-cashmere-brands-prices.md): What makes cashmere expensive, and when the cheap kind is fine.
