# Jeans Sizing by Brand

145 pairs of jeans from 96 labels, measured flat with the label in hand, in stock and sold. The same number on the label does not mean the same fit: at W33 we listed Silver as UK 12–14 and True Religion as UK 16.

Labels with three or more measured pairs get their own row. “Listed as” is the UK size on each pair’s own listing, judged from its flat measurements, never a calculation. Inches throughout; the waistband is measured flat across the top and doubled.

“Waistband vs label” is how much bigger the waistband measured than the number printed on the label, averaged over the brand’s pairs that carry a W on the label. Labels sized in US, junior or European numbers have no W to compare with: their row keeps the tape measurements only. Low-rise jeans sit on the hips, so the waistband usually measures over the label; the column shows by how much, brand by brand.

## How to use this

- Find your usual UK size in the “Listed as” column, then read the waist number next to it: that is the label to look for at that brand.
- When a label is not in the first table, use the second: the waistband range for that W across every brand is the safest guide.
- Before you buy, open the listing: each pair carries its own measurements, and [Will it fit?](https://kitanesque.co.uk/will-it-fit/) checks them against your size.
- To compare with a pair you own, measure it the same way: [how we measure jeans](https://kitanesque.co.uk/how-to-measure-jeans-waist-rise-inseam/).

## Questions people ask

**Do all jeans labels size the same?** No. The same number on the label does not mean the same fit: at W33 we listed Silver as UK 12–14 and True Religion as UK 16. This page compares 145 pairs from 96 labels, each one measured flat on the table with the label in hand. 57 of them carry a W on the label and 49 a US, junior or European size; for the other 39 the label size is not on record, so they count for their tape measurements only.

**What UK size is a Silver W26?** On the 2 Silver W26 pairs we measured, the waistband was 27″ round, with a rise of 8–8.5″. We listed them as a UK 8. Jeans sit on the hips, so the waistband measures more than the natural waist it is made for.

**What UK size is a Miss Me W26?** On the 2 Miss Me W26 pairs we measured, the waistband was 28–29″ round, with a rise of 7″. We listed them as a UK 6–8. Jeans sit on the hips, so the waistband measures more than the natural waist it is made for.

**How low did Y2K jeans sit?** On the 89 Y2K pairs we have measured, the median front rise is 8.5″; 29 of them sit under 8″, the lowest at 6″. Our tape, laid flat, not the brand's chart.

**Where do these numbers come from?** From our own listings. Every pair of jeans we sell is measured flat before it goes online, and this page reads those measurements straight from the catalogue, sold pairs included. It rebuilds itself when a pair is added or sold; the newest pair in it was listed on 26 November 2026.
